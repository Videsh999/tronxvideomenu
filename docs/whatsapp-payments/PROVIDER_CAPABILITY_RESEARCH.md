# TRONX Restaurant OS — Provider Capability Research

Research date: **7 November 2026 (IST)**. Scope: India, multi-tenant restaurant SaaS; each restaurant's own merchant account and settlement bank account. Research only: **no accounts created, no credentials accessed, no payment requests, messages, or money movements performed**. Context: `FOUNDER_ORDER_2026-11-07.md` in this directory.

## 1. How to read this report

- **DOCUMENTED** means an official documentation or pricing page explicitly describes the capability; it does not mean TRONX has tested it or has contractual access.
- **UNVERIFIED** means the examined official material does not establish the particular claim. It does **not** mean unsupported. Never implement an unknown as a guaranteed capability.
- **NOT TESTED** applies to every integration in this report. Merchant eligibility, commercial approval, credentials, production enablement and contract-specific settlement are **UNVERIFIED for TRONX**.
- Amount prefill, non-editable amount in an app, and provider-side rejection of a wrong amount are different guarantees. A URI containing `am` is not sufficient proof of enforcement. The table distinguishes them.
- Recommendations and proposed controls below are TRONX design judgments, not representations of provider guarantees. Provider facts have official source links. Search summaries were used for discovery, not to fill gaps; no third-party rate cards or provider blogs are relied upon.

## 3. Razorpay — detailed findings

### QR / links / partials

- Create `type=upi_qr`, `usage=single_use`, `fixed_amount=true`, `payment_amount` in currency subunits. Official text: a transaction less than or greater than `payment_amount` is not allowed. This is the strongest reviewed provider-side exact-amount statement. API calls need QR feature activation through support. [Create QR](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/qr-codes/create/)
- `POST /v1/payments/qr_codes/{qr_id}/close` is documented. However, the close reference describes a 15-minute minimum / up to 45-day single-use expiry, while the current create reference says 2-minute minimum, 2-hour maximum/default and server override. **Expiry contract consistency UNVERIFIED:** follow returned `close_by`, pin docs/API behavior and obtain clarification before promising a timeout. [Close](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/qr-codes/close) [Create](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/qr-codes/create/)
- Standard Payment Links support `accept_partial` and `first_min_partial_amount`; `payment_link.partially_paid` does not apply to UPI Payment Links. Do not label a fixed QR “partial-pay enabled”; instead create separate exact instalment requests after an explicitly chosen partial amount. [Partials](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-links/partial-payments) [Link events](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/payment-links)
- UPI Intent is a documented payment method. Its existence is not evidence that a hand-built UPI intent has the QR API's amount enforcement. [Intent](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payment-methods/upi/upi-intent)

### Webhooks / status / refunds

- Webhook verification: HMAC-SHA256 of the **raw body**, keyed by webhook secret, compared against `X-Razorpay-Signature`. Keep historical secrets for retried old events where applicable. [Validation](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/validate-test)
- QR events: `qr_code.created`, `qr_code.credited`, `qr_code.closed`. The closed event is **not triggered for auto-expiry**; therefore absence of that event is not proof the request is active. [QR events](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/qr-codes)
- Non-2xx responses retry with exponential backoff for 24 hours; continued failure disables the webhook. Duplicate/out-of-order delivery is expected; event identifier is `x-razorpay-event-id`. [Best practices](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/best-practices/)
- QR APIs list fetch QR, fetch payments for QR and other lookup APIs; payment APIs provide payment retrieval; refunds API supports refunds of captured payments, including specified amounts. [QR APIs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/qr-codes/apis) [Payment APIs](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/payments/apis) [Refunds](https://razorpay.com/docs/api/refunds)

### Sandbox / SaaS settlement

- Official QR guide says QR made in Test Mode through the PG flow disappears after two seconds and **only Live Mode QR can be scanned**. **Full no-money QR-scan E2E simulation UNVERIFIED / constrained**, even though test webhooks can be validated. Do not spend real money to satisfy the Founder's sandbox requirement. [QR test caveat](https://razorpay.com/docs/payments/qr-codes/create) [Test webhooks](https://razorpay.com/docs/webhooks/validate-test)
- Technology Partner OAuth allows authorisation by each sub-merchant, a unique access token to act on that merchant's resources, and an `account.app.authorization_revoked` event. Platform-specific terms explicitly include QR/link creation and initiating refunds with merchant consent. **TRONX approval, requested OAuth scopes, and QR availability under those scopes UNVERIFIED** until partner onboarding. [OAuth](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/technology-partners/onboard-businesses/integrate-oauth/integration-steps) [Platform terms](https://razorpay.com/s/terms/partners/aggregator-and-platform/)
- Official fund-flow examples accept the payment on the sub-merchant's Razorpay account and settle the remainder to that merchant's bank. The optional Control of Funds product adds platform/third-party fees and linked accounts. **Proposed TRONX choice:** no Route, no Control of Funds, no platform fee deduction from restaurant takings; charge SaaS subscriptions separately. A no-split merchant-direct contractual configuration still requires confirmation. [Fund flow](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/technology-partners/control-of-funds/)

## 4. Cashfree — detailed findings

### QR / links / partials

- Order Pay uses a previously created order's `payment_session_id`; `payment_method.upi.channel=qrcode` requests a base64 QR, while `channel=link` is mobile UPI Intent. API exposes `transaction_expiry_time`. **The reviewed API does not explicitly promise an uneditable QR amount or rejection of altered amounts. Exact-amount enforcement UNVERIFIED.** [Order Pay](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/payments/pay)
- Current Order Pay prose requires S2S enablement, and for seamless non-card methods a GST return showing annual turnover above ₹1 crore. Some schema client-environment descriptions differ from page prose. **Partner/small-restaurant exemptions, hosted-checkout alternative access and exact eligibility UNVERIFIED**; do not design unconditional in-POS QR generation for every small outlet. [Order Pay](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/payments/pay)
- Terminate Order requests `order_status=TERMINATED`; response can be `TERMINATION_REQUESTED`, and successful active payments can prevent termination. This is not a guaranteed immediate cancellation of an in-flight bank payment. A dedicated QR close API is **UNVERIFIED**. [Terminate Order](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/orders/terminate-order)
- General payment links permit multiple partial payments with a minimum amount. The UPI-only link instructions explicitly say partial payments are not allowed. A link can be cancelled; use transaction/order status, not `link_status` alone, because the FAQ warns an EXPIRED link can coexist with a successful settled payment. [Payment links FAQ](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/help/payment-links/faqs/faqs)

### Webhooks / status / refunds

- PG signature: `Base64(HMAC-SHA256(x-webhook-timestamp + rawBody, clientSecret))`, compare with `x-webhook-signature`; do not parse/re-serialize before verification. Secret rotation must accommodate in-flight events. [Signatures](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/webhooks/payloads-and-signatures)
- Payment event family includes success, failed, user-dropped; refund, settlement and link event families also exist. Retry policy defaults to three retries at 2, 10 and 30 minutes; custom policies are configurable. Delivery is at least once; newer versions expose `x-idempotency-header`. [Events/retries](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/webhooks/overview) [Payment events](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/payments/webhooks) [Refund events](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/refunds/webhooks)
- `GET /orders/{order_id}/payments` returns attempts; `GET` payment-by-ID is also documented. `POST /orders/{order_id}/refunds` accepts `refund_amount`, refund reference and refund speed. [Payments](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/payments/get-payments-for-an-order) [By ID](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/payments/get-payment-by-id) [Refund](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/refunds/create-refund)
- **Do not reuse PG verification for partner onboarding events:** the onboarding reference describes different `x-cashfree-*` headers and signature conventions. Pin and validate that product's exact SDK/formula independently; its `MERCHANT_ONBOARDING_STATUS` includes merchant ID and onboarding status. [Onboarding webhooks](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/platforms/latest/merchant-onboarding/webhooks)

### Sandbox / SaaS settlement

- Sandbox simulates UPI and real UPI apps do not launch; official troubleshooting recommends Cashfree's UPI simulator. Sandbox webhooks can be tested. **Exact QR-specific wrong-amount / replay / expiry simulation coverage UNVERIFIED**; the general simulator is not proof of every case. [SDK testing](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/integration-troubleshooting/sdk-ts) [Webhooks](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/api-reference/payments/latest/webhooks/overview)
- Embedded onboarding creates merchant profiles, collects/verifies KYC and tracks CREATED/ACTIVE/REJECTED/SUSPENDED. Gateway integration uses merchant-scoped partner authentication and adds merchant identity to partner payment webhooks. This is a suitable documented SaaS pattern; actual TRONX access is **UNVERIFIED**. [Onboarding](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/partners/embedded/embedded-merchant-onboarding) [Embedded PG](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/partners/embedded/integration/gateway-integration)
- Settlement reconciliation reports expose amounts credited to the merchant bank, UTRs, fees, taxes, refunds and transaction references. **Require a written merchant-by-merchant settlement contract**; do not infer it solely from a partner API header. Cashfree Easy Split is explicitly a marketplace collect/deduct/split product and is **not the proposed TRONX flow**. [Settlement report](https://www.cashfree.com/docs/partners/embedded/reports/settlement-recon-reports) [Pricing / Easy Split explanation](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-gateway-charges/)

## 5. PhonePe — detailed findings

### Product boundary and QR

- **Offline DQR is a separate documented integration.** `/v3/qr/init` takes merchant/store/terminal identity, amount in paise and expiry; has UAT/production hosts and request `X-VERIFY=SHA256(base64Payload + path + saltKey) + ### + saltIndex`. `X-PROVIDER-ID` is documented for provider-onboarded merchants. That is an API-request signature, **not proof of callback signature validation**. [DQR Init](https://developer.phonepe.com/offline-integration/dynamic-qr-solution/dqr-init-api)
- DQR FAQ states amount is pre-populated, QR unique per transaction, and cannot be scanned again after payment initiation even if that attempt fails. `expiresIn` is seconds; expiry produces `REQUEST_EXPIRED`. It does **not explicitly establish rejection of an edited/wrong amount across payer apps**. **Exact enforcement UNVERIFIED.** [FAQ](https://developer.phonepe.com/offline-integration/faqs/dynamic-qr-flow)
- Offline cancel API handles DQR/collect requests; if payment already completed it cannot cancel. Treat this as a race requiring status reconciliation. [Cancel](https://developer.phonepe.com/offline-integration/cancel-payment-request-api)
- PG Create Payment Link uses order amount in paise, expiry and end-merchant header for partner integrations. Offline Intent Init is also documented. **Do not assume the offline intent API and PG V2 share a contract or credentials.** [PG links](https://developer.phonepe.com/payment-gateway/payment-links/api-reference-payment-links/create-payment-links) [Offline intent](https://developer.phonepe.com/offline-integration/intent-generation-solution/intent-init-api)
- **Native partial collections UNVERIFIED:** the reviewed PG link API contains no explicit partial-payment fields. General product copy mentions partial options but also says links are single-use/single-transaction. Under this research's docs-only standard, do not promise repeat partial payment on one link. Proposed workaround within the application: separate exact instalment requests, conditional on verified payment and amount checks. [API](https://developer.phonepe.com/payment-gateway/payment-links/api-reference-payment-links/create-payment-links) [Official product FAQ—ambiguity, not a capability guarantee](https://business.phonepe.com/payment-link)

### Webhooks / status / refunds / test

- **PG V2** supports HMAC authentication (checksum secret/key ID and `x-phonepe-checksum-signature`) or `Authorization=SHA256(username:password)`. Events: `checkout.order.completed`, `checkout.order.failed`, `pg.refund.completed`, `pg.refund.failed`. Use `event` and root `payload.state`; duplicates are possible. The page describes HMAC but does not specify its complete digest/encoding in the reviewed text: **precise HMAC algorithm/encoding UNVERIFIED**; use a confirmed official SDK/contract, not guessed crypto. **PG V2 retry schedule UNVERIFIED.** [PG webhook reference](https://developer.phonepe.com/payment-gateway/website-integration/standard-checkout/api-integration/api-reference/webhook)
- **Offline callbacks**: terminal success/fail, HTTPS endpoint, base64 JSON; page describes three retries specifically for static QR callbacks. **Do not extrapolate three retries to DQR or PG V2. Offline DQR callback authentication / retry contract UNVERIFIED** in reviewed callback material. [Offline callbacks](https://developer.phonepe.com/offline-integration/response-capturing-api/server-to-server-callback)
- PG Order Status API exists; PG integration docs contain Initiate Refund; offline refund API describes amount-based refunds. **Confirm the refund product and merchant context rather than calling an offline refund API for a PG payment.** [PG status](https://developer.phonepe.com/payment-gateway/website-integration/standard-checkout/api-integration/api-reference/order-status) [PG API navigation/refund reference](https://developer.phonepe.com/payment-gateway/website-integration/standard-checkout/api-integration/api-reference/webhook) [Offline refund](https://developer.phonepe.com/offline-integration/integrated-static-qr/refund-api)
- Current PG UAT docs explicitly simulate success/failure/pending without real transactions. UAT QR opens a browser simulation after scanning; UPI Intent uses test-app templates. **This confirms PG sandbox QR/intent simulation, not offline DQR entitlement or real bank enforcement.** [UAT](https://developer.phonepe.com/payment-gateway/uat-testing-go-live/uat-sandbox)
- Partner API merchant headers are documented; **a general OAuth connect/disconnect flow, automated multi-restaurant KYC API access and merchant-direct settlement terms for TRONX are UNVERIFIED**. No split-settlement product is recommended for this brief. [Partner header](https://developer.phonepe.com/payment-gateway/payment-links/api-reference-payment-links/create-payment-links)

## 9. Settlement, partner versus split model, and RBI boundary

### Official regulatory position

The **RBI Regulation of Payment Aggregators Directions, 2025**, issued 15 September 2025, is the current direction examined here—not the superseded 2020 text. It defines:

- **PA**, paragraph 4(i): aggregates customer payments and subsequently settles collected funds to merchants.
- **PG**, paragraph 4(j): technology infrastructure routing/processing payments **without handling funds**.
- Non-bank PA authorisation, paragraph 5(b); PG excluded from this MD's scope under paragraph 10(g), with baseline technology recommendations encouraged.
- Merchant CDD and merchant bank-account validation, paragraph 13; non-bank PA merchant funds held in separate escrow, paragraph 16. Merchant settlement timing is **as agreed fairly/transparently in the PA–merchant agreement**, not a universal T+1 guarantee.

Source for all bullets: [RBI 2025 Master Direction, paragraphs 4, 5, 10, 13, 16 and repeal provisions](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12896).

**Proposed TRONX boundary (architecture recommendation, not a legal opinion):** Customer → authorised provider's merchant payment processing → that restaurant's verified bank; TRONX holds only references/ledger data and acts with scoped merchant consent. No TRONX merchant collection account for restaurant sales, no pooled balance, no wallet, no payout redistribution, no platform split/commission deduction. Technology partner terminology such as “aggregator” in vendor docs is not proof TRONX is an RBI-authorised PA. **TRONX's legal classification is UNVERIFIED** until counsel and provider review the actual contract and operational flow against the official definitions above.

Partner/OAuth is delegated API access, not automatically a fund-flow guarantee. Require written confirmation for **each provider** that the merchant is the restaurant, restaurant KYC/bank is verified, sales settle to it, TRONX never possesses/controls pooled funds, and refund/chargeback responsibility is assigned. Do not assume Route/Easy Split/split settlements make TRONX handle funds personally; those can be provider-managed, but they introduce multi-party allocations/control unnecessary for this order. Razorpay Control of Funds and Cashfree Easy Split explicitly describe those allocations. [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/docs/partners/technology-partners/control-of-funds/) [Cashfree](https://www.cashfree.com/payment-gateway-charges/)
